We store school operations data so you can collect fees and message parents responsibly — and we verify schools before they go live.

What we collect

School profile and contacts, staff accounts, student roster details you enter (including parent phones for WhatsApp and fees), fee invoices and payment records, and verification documents you upload when applying.

Why we collect it

To run your school workspace: fee collection (including M-Pesa), parent messaging, attendance and staff collaboration, and to confirm that people applying for a school are authorised to represent it.

School verification

New schools are not instant. Applications are reviewed by our team (target: 2 business days). Until approval, applicants only see a verification-pending screen and can still switch to other schools they belong to.

Payments & secrets

Parent fee payments go to your school’s Paybill via Safaricom Daraja. Kora reconciles callbacks into your ledger. API secrets are encrypted at rest. Platform subscription billing is separate from parent fee collection.

Your control

School admins manage staff access, student records, and settings inside Kora. For data access or deletion requests related to your school, contact us through your account manager or the email on your application.

Kenya context

We design for Kenyan school operations (CBC-era structures, M-Pesa, WhatsApp). We aim to handle personal data carefully under applicable Kenyan data protection expectations. This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.
