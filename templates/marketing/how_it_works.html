You apply, we verify, then you run classes, fees, and WhatsApp from one place — not instant self-serve for fake campuses.

Sign up with your work email. Join a school by invitation, or apply to add a new one.

School details, term dates, and verification docs — registration or licence, your ID, and an authority letter. Review usually takes up to 2 business days.
