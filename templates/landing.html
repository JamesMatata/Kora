{# Three product stories — big type, no eyebrows #}

Money, messages, and people — without five tools taped together.

Fee agent

A WhatsApp agent follows up on outstanding fees, handles routine replies — pay, promise a date, or talk to the school — and hands off when a human should take over.

Ledger & M-Pesa

Paybill and STK land in student balances. Statements, defaulters, and receipts stay on the same ledger the reminders use — so WhatsApp never invents a number.

Classes & staff

Grades, streams, roster import, attendance, and roles for admins and teachers who wear both hats.

  • WhatsApp replies

    Parents can text in anytime. The agent handles routine fee work; staff claim the thread when judgment is needed.
